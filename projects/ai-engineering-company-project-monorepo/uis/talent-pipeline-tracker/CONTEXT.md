# TrackFlow — Company Context

TrackFlow is a mid-sized logistics SaaS company (~180 employees) that helps carriers
and shippers coordinate freight in real time. The People & Talent team is currently
running an aggressive hiring campaign to staff a new Operations & Product pod, and
they need a lightweight internal tool to keep the process from unraveling.

## What this tool is

This is an internal People & Talent workspace for the TrackFlow hiring team.
It is not customer-facing — every screen assumes the viewer is a TrackFlow
recruiter or hiring manager.

## Terminology used in the UI

- "Candidates" — every applicant to a TrackFlow open role.
- "Pipeline stage" — where the candidate sits in TrackFlow's hiring flow
  (Pending, Under review, Personal interview, Technical interview,
   Offer presented).
- "Status" — the recruiter's high-level disposition
  (Received, In progress, Selected, Discarded).
- "Notes" — internal-only recruiter notes; never visible to the candidate.
- "Register candidate" / "Edit candidate" — for adding or correcting applicant
  records that came in outside the standard application form.

## Voice

Direct, calm, useful. This tool exists so the People team can move faster on
Monday morning — no marketing copy, no motivational language.
